    Bargaining power

    Obsolescing bargain

    Before you invest, the government needs your money. Once the factory is built, your money needs the government. Every euro you sink tips the balance (slide 5; more in Lecture 8).

    How the model works, and the research behind it

    Mechanics

    • VoltDrive commits €500M. The business plan adds €60M of value a year (€42M in a joint venture, where the partner keeps 30%). Faster approvals add value early: Solaria approves in 4–6 months, Valoria in 12–18.
    • Valoria has frequent, smaller events every year (court challenges, regional elections, new rules). Solaria has quiet years and rare, sharper shocks. The dashed line replays your exact choices in the other country.
    • Arbitration protection (€10M) unlocks arbitration options. It is worth most where local courts enforce awards. Political risk insurance (€20M) pays up to 90% of an expropriation loss, but not for general tax laws.
    • Every €1 sunk in the host country shifts bargaining power towards the government. Diversifying and credible protections shift it back.
    • A joint venture brings permits and political cover, which matter most where formal rules are weak. It also gives the partner access to more of your code.
    • Country profiles average real 2025 scores (EIU Democracy Index, World Bank WGI, Transparency International CPI). All payoffs are teaching assumptions, not forecasts.
